Privacy Policy

The Last Map · Last updated: 10 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game The Last Map (the "Game", package name com.thelastmap.game) handles your information. The Game is developed by Maaz Khawaja (the "Developer").

1. Information we collect

The Game does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. There are no user accounts, no logins, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking tools.

2. Data stored on your device

The Game may save game progress and settings locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to the Developer. Uninstalling the Game deletes this data.

3. Third-party services

The Game is built with the Godot Engine and does not include any third-party SDKs that collect data. The Game is distributed through Google Play. Google's handling of data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Children's privacy

The Game is intended for users aged 13 and over. Because the Game collects no personal data, no personal information from children is collected.

5. Your rights

Because no personal data is collected, there is no personal data to access, correct or delete. If you have questions about your rights under the GDPR or other laws, please contact us.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
